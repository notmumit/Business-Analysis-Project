# Ridgeway Cycle Parts — Requirements Documentation

This folder contains three complementary requirements documents for a conceptual specialist e-commerce project.

## Documents

- `Ridgeway_BRD.docx` — Business Requirements Document: business context, problem/opportunity, objectives, stakeholders, scope, high-level business requirements, proposed success measures, risks and open questions.
- `Ridgeway_PRD.docx` — Product Requirements Document: product vision, working personas, scope, product capabilities, user journeys, roadmap and product decisions.
- `Ridgeway_FRD.docx` — Functional Requirements Document: functional requirements, proposed acceptance criteria, business rules, non-functional considerations, integrations and QA approach.

## Source materials

The documents are based on:
- `Ridgeway_USA_WBS.xlsx` (sheets: `UI_v2` and `Phase 1 V1`)
- `Ridgeway_Sprint_Planning.xlsx` (Backlog, Sprint 01–08, Read Me)

## Important scope and accuracy notes

- This is a self-directed portfolio project, not evidence of a real client engagement or production implementation.
- The original workbooks identify client, team-member and card-link information as fictional sample data. Estimates are ballpark/demo values.
- The WBS contains both `Phase 1 V1` and `UI_v2`; the source does not establish which one is formally approved or whether one supersedes the other.
- Features are described as in-scope or planned, not necessarily implemented.
- Proposed priorities, user journeys, business metrics, business rules and acceptance criteria have been added to make the documents useful for analysis and review. They must be validated before being treated as approved requirements.
- No complete business case, detailed API contracts, full data model, signed-off policies, or feature-level acceptance criteria were present in the source workbooks.
