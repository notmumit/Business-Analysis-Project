# Business Analysis Project

This folder contains three complementary requirements documents for a conceptual specialist e-commerce project.

## Documents

- `BRD.docx` — Business Requirements Document: business context, problem/opportunity, objectives, stakeholders, scope, high-level business requirements, proposed success measures, risks and open questions.
- `PRD.docx` — Product Requirements Document: product vision, working personas, scope, product capabilities, user journeys, roadmap and product decisions.
- `FRD.docx` — Functional Requirements Document: functional requirements, proposed acceptance criteria, business rules, non-functional considerations, integrations and QA approach.

## Source materials

The documents are based on:
- `WBS.xlsx` (sheets: `UI_v2` and `Phase 1 V1`)
- `Sprint Planning.xlsx` (Backlog, Sprint 01–08, Read Me)
